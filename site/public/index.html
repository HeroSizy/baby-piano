Made for little ears

Built around the sound

At six months, babies mostly listen. So the sound comes first, and everything else stays out of the way.

do re mi sol la

There are no wrong notes

Every key plays from one gentle scale: do re mi sol la, from deep bass to tinkling top. Mash ten keys at once and it still sounds like music. Grown-ups can switch to seven notes or all twelve.

Soft where it matters

High notes play quieter than low ones. A slap across a dozen keys plays only a few. The computer's volume stays capped while playing, even if headphones or a speaker connect midway.

A calm screen, or fireworks

By default the screen stays dark, because the baby is listening, not watching. Or turn on fireworks and every note bursts into color, like the piano above.

Good to know

  • Mac: the first time, allow Mash Piano under System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility. That is what lets it lock the keyboard. It records nothing.
  • Laptops: turn on "Also lock the mouse and trackpad" so little palms can't click anything.
  • iPhone and iPad: turn on Guided Access, then triple-click the side button while it plays. Android: use App pinning.
