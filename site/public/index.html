Why it exists

It started with an old keyboard

Our baby loved slapping a spare mechanical keyboard, so it became a toy. But on a real computer every slap did something: apps quit, windows switched, shortcuts fired, and nothing sounded nice.

Mash Piano turns that keyboard, or a phone or tablet, into an instrument. Every key plays a piano note that fits with the others, and nothing else on the device reacts.

What it does

Every tap sounds lovely

Babies at this age mostly listen, so Mash Piano is built around sound first, and keeps everything else out of the way.

🎵

Always in harmony

Every note comes from one gentle scale (five, seven or all twelve notes, you choose), so any mash of keys sounds pleasant together.

🎹

A real grand piano

Recorded from a Yamaha C5 grand (Salamander Grand Piano), not a synthesized beep.

👂

Kind to small ears

High notes are softer, a slap on a dozen keys plays only a few, and the volume stays capped while playing.

⌨️

The whole keyboard plays

On a computer every key has its own note. Nothing quits, switches apps or triggers a system shortcut.

🖐️

Big keys for every finger

On phones and tablets the screen becomes rows of big colored keys, played with as many fingers as fit.

🎆

Fireworks, if you like

The screen can stay calm and dark, or every note can burst into a firework in the color of its pitch.

Download

Free on every device you have

Same app everywhere. On a computer it locks the keyboard so a spare one becomes a safe toy.

iPhone and iPad

For this device

Turn on Guided Access (Settings › Accessibility › Guided Access), then triple-click the side button while the piano plays, to keep little hands in the app.

Get it on the App Store

Mac

For this device

Signed and notarized by Apple. Open the disk image and drag Mash Piano into Applications. The first time, allow it under Privacy & Security › Accessibility so it can lock the keyboard.

Run the installer; no administrator rights needed. While playing, the Win key, Alt+Tab and Alt+F4 are blocked.

For parents

Set up in seconds, out in three

The baby can't get out. You can, either way below.

Set it up

Pick a volume and press Try it. On a laptop, you can also lock the mouse and trackpad.

Hand it over

Press Start playing. It goes full screen, and the keyboard, or the screen, only plays notes.

Stop playing

Hold the top-right corner for 3 seconds, or on a keyboard type quitpiano.
