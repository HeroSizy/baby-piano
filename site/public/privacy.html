Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Mash Piano collects no data.

  • It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • It never connects to the internet.
  • The keys a child presses are turned into sound on the device and are not recorded, stored or sent anywhere.
  • The only things it saves are its own settings, such as the volume, on the device itself.
  • On a Mac or Windows computer, it asks for the permission to lock the keyboard (Accessibility on macOS) only so that a child’s key presses cannot quit the app or trigger system shortcuts.

This website keeps no cookies and runs no analytics.

Questions: open an issue at github.com/HeroSizy/baby-piano/issues.
