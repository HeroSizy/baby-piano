**Description**

Mash Piano started with an old mechanical keyboard at home. Our baby loved slapping it, so we made it play music.

Hand your iPhone or iPad to your little one: the screen becomes rows of big, bright keys, and every tap plays a soft grand piano note. All the notes come from one pentatonic scale, so whatever tiny hands do, it always sounds pleasant together.

• Big colorful keys that fill the screen, made for babies and toddlers
• Play with every finger at once
• Real grand piano sound (Salamander Grand Piano)
• Gentle on small ears: high notes are softer, and a slap on many keys plays only a few
• A firework of color bursts from every finger
• Full screen while playing, with the status bar and edges out of the way
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, no data collected, works offline

Keep your child in the app: turn on Guided Access (Settings › Accessibility › Guided Access), then triple-click the side button while the piano plays.

Grown-ups: hold the top-right corner for 3 seconds to return to the settings.

Also on Mac and Windows, where it turns a spare keyboard into a piano that can't quit, switch apps or open shortcuts: https://github.com/HeroSizy/baby-piano
